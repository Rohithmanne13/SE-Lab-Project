# The PARA method (Tiago Forte)

## Summary

Tiago Forte's article "The PARA Method: The Simple System for Organizing Your Digital Life in Seconds" (Forte Labs, published 24 February 2023, updated 15 April 2026). PARA is the structure the course prescribes for our `brain/` vault: four top-level categories organised by actionability.

## Key points

- Projects: short-term efforts with a specific goal (e.g. "write research report").
- Areas: areas of responsibility that need ongoing attention and do not end (e.g. health, finances, engineering).
- Resources: topics of ongoing interest or learning (e.g. notetaking, web design).
- Archives: anything from the other three categories that is no longer active but worth keeping (completed projects, inactive areas, resources no longer of interest).
- Key principle: organise by the projects and goals you are committed to now ("organize by actionability"), not by broad school-style subjects, so everything needed for a project is in one place.
- The system must stay simple enough that maintaining it does not consume the time it should save.
- An item that never ends (e.g. "strategic planning") is an area, not a project; breaking areas into concrete projects makes workload and progress visible.
